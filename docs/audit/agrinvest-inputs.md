2. **Account access:** hosting and domains for agrinvest.ro and agrinvestshop.ro (DNS), Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Ads (for Keyword Planner), Google Business Profile.
3. **Confirmation of the figures register** (`figures-register.md`): the rows still to confirm; the capacities per site add up to 252,000 t, while the confirmed total is 275,000 t.
4. **For each of the 17 bases:** county, address, GPS coordinates, phone, contact person, hours, services (reception, drying, cleaning, weighing), photos, **indicative storage and drying tariffs** (lei/t/month). No competitor publishes tariffs, so this is a clear Google opportunity.
5. **Permission to use the YouTube videos** (the 2022 brand film, the Agro TV appearances) on the site.
6. **Real photos:** bases, team, fleet, fields, events. No stock photos.
   - **The logo as a vector file** (SVG, AI or PDF) and any brand guidelines. Until then we use a redrawn trace of the current logo.
   - **Who receives each type of enquiry** from the site (selling grain, storage, inputs, financing, logistics, partnerships, careers): name, role, phone, email, and consent to show the phone on the site.
   - **The privacy policy** for the forms (GDPR), approved by the data protection officer (dpo@agrinvest.ro). The page exists on the site without final text.
   - **A review of the core page copy** (phase 2.2): the Romanian text is a draft rewritten from the old site; every page has a summary, an FAQ and figures from the register.

8. **Technical sheets and safety data sheets (PDF)** from the manufacturers.

    - **Prices to check:** Wing P 10L (720 vs 1,450 lei), the two AVENUE 800 kg products (2,250 and 2,800 lei) and Corida 75 WG 0.1 kg (1,938 lei, that is 19,380 lei/kg).

    - **Maize and sunflower seed:** missing from the shop although they are priority keywords. Do you sell them?
    - **Who receives orders** and input enquiries: name, role, phone, email.

### Product enrichment (phase 3.3)

20. **Brand, manufacturer and label for each crop-protection product.** `product-verification.md` lists, per product, what is still missing: registration number, validity date, waiting period, toxicity classification (GHS) and buffer zones. The old shop's dose tables are not used: send the manufacturer labels or technical sheets (PDF) and the doses are added straight away.
21. **Do your licences cover online sale?** (Owner: Agrinvest holds all the required licences.) OUG 53/2025 ties online trade to the authorisation holder or its approved distributors: OUG 53/2025 limits online trade in plant-protection products to the authorisation holder and its approved distributors (see `oug-53-2025-summary.md`). A written confirmation per manufacturer is needed before checkout opens.
22. **Three products have no valid authorisation in the ANF decisions:** Tazer 250 SC (withdrawn 30.06.2020), Kaiso Sorbie 5 EG (withdrawn 31.01.2025) and Closer (greenhouse use only since 19.11.2022). Do you still hold stock, and under which authorisation?
    22b. **Which products carry the GHS06 pictogram?** They cannot be sold online under OUG 53/2025.
23. **Jolpolt** is registered as an insecticide (second name of Delmur, deltamethrin 25 g/L), not an adjuvant. Please check the pack and send a photo of the label; the old photo is marked "Adjuvant".
24. **Mix-ups in the old shop:** Corida 75 WG is a cereal herbicide (tribenuron-methyl), not an insecticide. Zanzibar and SU Ellen are filed as barley but the old text says wheat: which species are they? Stabilan: is it 750 g/L or the registered 400 g/L product? Mospilan: 20 SP or 20 SG?
25. **Products that may not be plant-protection products:** Seed Max and Ympact Completo are moved to fertilisers; Prevatect and Kappa-O have no registration found. Please confirm what they are and send the labels before they claim fungicidal effect.
26. **Seed varieties:** send the supplier sheets (or the official catalogue entries) for Zanzibar, SU Ellen, Cardinal, Sorrial, Solindo, Sofru, Rubisko, PG102, Asterion and Apache. The old texts for these are generic templates and are not used.
27. **Price basis for bulk fertilisers:** the old pages say "price per kilogram" for urea, ammonium nitrate and DAP while the packs are 600–1,000 kg. Are 1,650 / 2,800 / 2,550 lei the price of the whole pack?
28. **Photos:** the old photos are kept with rights pending; Jolpolt's photo says "Adjuvant".
