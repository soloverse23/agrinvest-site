## Terms found

- Lei only.
- Up to 7 years for investment credit on the old page. **New investment credit: up to 10 years** ([agrointel, 03.06.2026](https://agrointel.ro/351887/agrinvest-credit-ifn-sa-schimba-regulile-in-agricultura-credite-pana-la-10-ani-si-reducere-a-dobanzii-de-pana-la-10-an-pentru-cei-disciplinati); CEO on [Agro TV, 16.06.2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQKJGx5ZAbU)). It covers machinery and equipment, crop and livestock farms, storage, irrigation and agricultural land.
- **Margin reduction on that credit:** up to 10% of the interest margin, for respecting the contract and the repayment schedule. The headline's "10%/an" overstates it.
- Repayment agreed with the farmer, **at most 2 instalments a year**, aligned with the autumn and spring harvests.
- **Speed, three versions:** 24 h reply and 3 days to money (old page, 2021 and 2025 press); about 3 days and 2 more for payment (CEO, Nov 2025); 24 h and 24 h (CEO and agrointel, June 2026).
- Products tailored to each client's financial analysis, guarantees and seasonality.
- **Guarantees accepted:** promissory notes, crops, agricultural machinery, APIA subsidies, grain purchase contracts, real-estate mortgage.
- **Eligible borrowers:** PFA, II, IF, SRL, SA, agricultural companies. The main target is small and medium farmers with limited access to bank credit.
- **Not published anywhere:** amounts (min/max), interest, fees, document list, process steps.

## Links to the rest of the group

- **Logistics:** part of the grain Agrinvest receives comes from farmers repaying credit in kind at harvest (flota-proprie page). This "pay with your harvest" mechanism is a strong message for `/finantare/inputuri-plata-la-recolta`.

## Competitor benchmark: Agricover Credit IFN

- Named products: Creditul Instant, Motorină cu plata la recoltă, Hectar Plus, Creditul Avans Forward, Creditul Șeptel Plus, Creditul pentru Irigații.
- **Card Fermier:** a pay-at-harvest card, up to 250,000 lei (was 150,000), no monthly instalments, no guarantees, for 10 ha or more.
- **Published limits:** working capital up to 12 months, investment up to 84 months; rate set by scoring.
- **Agricover Pay:** a digital platform.
- International funding (EFSE, IFC, EIF).
- Reported as a top 3 agricultural lender (6.61% share, year unclear).
- Sources: [Revista Ferma](https://revista-ferma.ro/de-ce-este-agricover-credit-ifn-un-finantator-diferit-pentru-fermieri/), [AgroInfo](https://agroinfo.ro/agricover-lanseaza-agricover-pay-prima-platforma-financiara-din-europa-dedicata-fermierilor/).

**Implication:** Agrinvest needs named, clearly explained products and online tools to compete. Its advantages are speed (24 h / 3 days), harvest-aligned instalments, grain and contracts accepted as guarantees, and a single group (inputs + grain + storage + finance).

## Market context: APIA certificate credit (2026)

- APIA issues certificates; banks and IFNs **that have an agreement with APIA and the guarantee funds** lend up to 90% of the certified subsidy.
- FGCR/FNGCIMM guarantee (one source says up to 80% of each loan: check). Interest: **banks ROBOR 6M + max. 2%, IFNs ROBOR 6M + max. 8%** (OMADR 703/2013, as APIA states it).
- In 2026 the scheme also covers livestock interventions.
- APIA pays the advance from 16 October.
- **Agrinvest's participation:** not in any list found; APIA's convention page could not be read on 2026-11-05 (SSL error). Press lists disagree and name one IFN at most.
- Sources: [Revista Ferma](https://revista-ferma.ro/cati-bani-puteti-lua-cu-adeverinta-apia-in-2026/), [financiarul.ro](https://financiarul.ro/agricultura/banii-de-la-apia-ajung-mai-devreme-la-fermieri-ce-dobanzi-percep-bancile/).

## What the IFN's own interviews add

Agro TV, checked in the auto-captions only so far. Check against the audio before use: the captions say "grupul Agricover" for Agrinvest and "80% pe an" for what must be a much lower rate.

- **June 2026 (CEO):** no standard scoring, analysis per client; the only integrated player in the market (a claim, not for the site); flexibility on cost, guarantees and repayment; insolvency does not protect from debts, and creditors are paid up to their guarantees; negotiates with machinery and irrigation suppliers for clients.
- **November 2025 (CEO, CFO):** enforcement against personal guarantors does not stop in insolvency; advice to sell about 30% on forward contracts, 30% at harvest and store the rest for 2–4 months.
- **April 2025 (CEO):** lends to agriculture only; land at about €8,000–12,000/ha takes over 20 years to pay back at current financing costs; 5–7 year term credit with seasonal instalments, and no other financing cost between them.
- **Insolvency recovery:** "1 in 10" (Nov 2025) and "under 5%" (June 2026). Not for publication without a source.
