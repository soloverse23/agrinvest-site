# OUG 53/2025 on plant protection products: summary for Agrinvest's lawyer

**This is a reading aid, not legal advice.** Source: Emergency Ordinance no. 53 of 2 October 2025 (Monitorul Oficial no. 908), as published on the ANF site (`https://anfdf.ro/download/ordonanta-de-urgenta-nr-53-din-2-octombrie-2025/`, a 16-page copy of the CTCE Intralegis text read on 21 October 2025). In force from 2 October 2025. Later amendments, the implementing orders and the transition rules were **not** checked. Article numbers are those of that copy; they should be verified against the current consolidated text.

**Owner's note (2026-11-04):** Agrinvest holds all the licences it needs. The checklist below therefore asks the lawyer to confirm, per manufacturer and per product, that the licences cover **online** sale, not whether Agrinvest is licensed at all.

## What it covers

Manufacture, packing, labelling, repacking, storage, trade, import, export, use and services with plant protection products (PPP) in Romania. It replaces Government Ordinance 4/1995 (art. 73).

## What matters for the shop

1. **Online trade is restricted to the authorisation holder** (or its legal representative in Romania) **and the distributors that holder has approved** (art. 40 para. 1). It is allowed **only on the website of those operators** (para. 2). A reseller that is not an approved distributor of the holder may not sell PPP online (listed as a contravention: art. 66, points on online trade by others, and on sale from other websites).
2. **Registration first:** an operator that wants to sell online must hold the registration certificate for trade (art. 4 para. 1 and art. 32), **publish the certificate on the site**, and **notify the Authority at least 30 days before** starting (art. 40 paras. 3–4).
3. **Delivery** of PPP sold online must use **authorised transport** (art. 40 para. 5).
4. **Banned online:** PPP with acute toxicity category 1, 2 or 3 carrying the **GHS06** (skull) pictogram (art. 40 para. 6). Trade and repacking of those products online is also stated to be a criminal offence, "trafic cu produse sau substanțe toxice" (Criminal Code art. 359), together with trade without a registration certificate (art. 65).
5. **Banned to minors** (art. 40 para. 7).
6. **Physical points of sale** need a registration certificate and a responsible person with a professional-training certificate and agricultural studies (art. 32). Each point of sale must be registered in its county (art. 66, fines of 10,000–15,000 lei are listed for such failures). Fines in art. 66 apply from 30 days after publication (art. 72).
7. **Seed:** selling seed treated with PPP is allowed only when treated with products authorised for that purpose and with a label naming the product, the active substances and the safety phrases (art. 57); selling seed treated with a product not authorised in Romania or another EU state is a contravention (art. 66).
8. **Professional users** keep a treatment register (art. 50), observe waiting periods before selling produce (art. 52) and may not spray bee-toxic products on flowering melliferous crops (art. 51). Buyers and users of GHS06 products need their own registration (arts. 53–55, 59).

## Questions to put to the lawyer

- Is Agrinvest the authorisation holder, or an **approved distributor in writing** of each holder whose products it sells online? Which of the 38 crop-protection products does that cover?
- Which of the 38 products carry the **GHS06** pictogram and acute toxicity 1–3? They cannot be sold through the cart; they could be listed for information with a contact form for in-store sale only. Labels and safety data sheets are needed to answer this (see `product-verification.md`).
- Does the shop need the **trade registration certificate** and the 30-day notice before checkout is opened (Phase 3.4)? Must the certificate appear on every page or in the footer?
- Does an order that "a consultant confirms by phone" count as online trade under art. 40?
- What are the rules for **delivery** with the own fleet and with couriers ("mijloace de transport autorizate")?
- Does the professional-use certificate check at checkout (3.4) still add anything, given the rules above?
- Products with no registration number found (listed in `product-verification.md`): may they be shown, and under which description? Some are described as fungicides, insecticides or adjuvants without a registration found.
- What do the implementing orders (due within 120 days of entry into force, art. 70) and the 12-month transition for old documents (art. 71) change for Agrinvest?
