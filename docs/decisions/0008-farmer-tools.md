# 0008 Farmer tools: public sources, stubbed sending, widgets everywhere

- Status: accepted (owner, 2026-11-10); sources checked in 5.1 (2026-11-10), see "Update" below
- Date: 2026-11-10

## Context

Phase 5 adds grain and input prices, diesel, weather and news (`docs/phases/05-farmer-tools.md`). The site has no email or WhatsApp sender, Agrinvest has not yet supplied its buy prices or diesel price, and the script budget has about 8 KB left. The owner also asked for the tools to appear as widgets across the site and for a lighter home page.

## Decision

- **Agrinvest's own prices come only from the admin** (`BuyPrices`, `DieselPrices`, published rows only). Until a row exists, pages show public market data and "price on request". No Agrinvest price is ever estimated.
- **Public data only from sources that allow reuse**, each named with its date on the page:
  - EU agri-food data portal: cereal and fertiliser prices
  - EU Weekly Oil Bulletin: the national diesel price
  - BRM weekly table
  - Open-Meteo forecasts
  - ANM warnings
  - RSS feeds: headline, source, date and link only

  A source whose terms don't allow this is dropped, never scraped.

- **Open-Meteo's free API is non-commercial.** The weather client takes a configurable base URL and API key, and the site goes live with weather only on a paid key or an approved alternative.
- **Alerts and the newsletter are built, but sending is stubbed.** Every message goes through one `sendMessage` interface into a staff-only `Outbox` with status `stubbed`. Subscriptions stay `pending` until a provider exists, because a confirmation message can't be sent yet. This is the same rule as `notifyLead`.
- **Tools are server-rendered widgets**, with client code only for the location button and inline calculator inputs, each in its own lazy chunk. Charts are server SVG, with no chart library. A `toolWidget` page block lets any block-built page, and later Phase 6 articles, embed a tool.
- **Home page: "Today first".** The full map and figures row are replaced on the home page by small teasers linking to `/baze` and `/despre/cifre`. A guides slot stays hidden until Phase 6 publishes articles.
- **Lighthouse runs once, at the end of the phase** (see 0003, update 2026-11-10).

## Consequences

- Alerts and newsletters reach no one until the owner chooses a provider. The Outbox shows what would have been sent.
- `check:tools` (5.8) must fail on stale series once `SITE_INDEXABLE=true`, because a dated price page with old data misleads.
- Between Lighthouse runs, LCP, CLS and TBT regressions are only caught at the end of the phase. Byte budgets cover the most common cause.

### Consequences of the check

- **No Romanian commodity-exchange series.** Without BRM, grain pages show the EU series for Romania and Agrinvest's own published prices. The 5.0 plan's `fetchBrm` job is not built.
- **No official weather warnings feed.** Weather pages compute their own frost and spraying flags from the forecast (5.5) and link to ANM for official warnings.
- **News starts with two feeds.** Revista Ferma (headlines only) and Agrointel. The owner can ask Agrimedia, Agro TV and BRM for written permission; each then becomes one entry in `src/lib/sources.ts`.
- **Hosting:** Vercel Hobby runs each cron at most once a day, within the hour. That suits weekly and monthly series; the schedules in `vercel.json` are all daily or weekly.

## Update: recheck and calculator sources (5.2, 2026-11-10)

- **EU agri-food API, still down.** Cereal (RO, 8 weeks): a timeout, then HTTP 500. Fertiliser: a gateway timeout. Oilseed spec and prices: HTTP 500 "SUSPENDED". The jobs and parsers stay as built; pages show "no EU prices yet" until data arrives. Check again at the start of 5.3.
- **Net-price calculator.** It never assumes a quality limit. Limits come from `Crops.quality` (Agrinvest's published reception limits) or from the farmer's contract. The moisture formula is cited to FAO's worked drying example. EU intervention limits (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1238) are not quoted, because EUR-Lex could not be read to verify them. Rules and sources are in `src/lib/prices/net-price-rules.ts`, each with a "checked" date for `check:tools`.

## Update: simpler finish (5.3–5.8, 2026-11-10)

The owner asked to finish Phase 5 without over-engineering: show the data we have on good SEO pages, link them well, and put them on the home page.

- **Weather is out of scope** (owner, 2026-11-10). No Open-Meteo integration ships; the 5.1 client stays unused.
- **News is shown unless hidden.** Headlines from the licence-checked feeds are public on arrival; staff hide what does not belong. The feeds carry only title, date, link and the excerpt each publisher allows, so nothing beyond the terms is shown.
- **No excise calculator yet.** It needs the current legal rates, checked and cited, and none could be verified. It waits for the owner's or an accountant's confirmation.
- **Home:** a "Today" row after the hero instead of the full "Today first" redesign.
